Design a modern industrial chemical company website UI for a brand called **Ambica Alum Industries**, founded in 1998.

The company manufactures and supplies different types of alum and aluminium sulphate used in water treatment, agriculture, pharma, cosmetics, paper mills, and industrial applications.

The goal of the website is to transform a traditional B2B supplier into a modern **D2C + B2B industrial e-commerce platform** that communicates:

Trust
Quality
Technical expertise
Fast response
Reliable delivery

The interface must feel **professional, clean, modern, and industrial**.
